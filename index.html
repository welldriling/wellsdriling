FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to drill a well?

The cost depends on the depth, diameter and geology of your land. After a site study we give you a clear, fixed quote — with no hidden costs afterwards.

How deep will my well need to be?

Depth depends on the water table under your land. Our geophysical study estimates the depth before drilling begins, so you know what to expect.

How long does drilling take?

Most wells are completed in 2 to 7 days, depending on depth and ground conditions. We confirm the schedule with you before we start.

Do you guarantee to find water?

We use geophysical studies to choose the drilling point with the best chance of water, and we discuss the results of the study with you honestly before any drilling starts.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Oujda and work across the whole Oriental Region — Berkane, Taourirt, Jerada, Figuig and beyond. Other regions on request.

Do you also install and maintain pumps?

Yes — we supply and install submersible pumps and pressure systems, and we offer maintenance and repair to keep your well working all year.
